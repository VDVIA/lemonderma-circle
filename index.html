FAQ

Do I need a big following?
No — the opposite. We look for emerging creators on the rise. We bet on potential and an eye for video, not follower count.
How do I climb, and how much do I earn?
Climb by total views or sales (whichever first) — reviewed monthly. You earn 30% commission on every sale you drive, at every tier — about double the usual 10–15%. The more you convert, the more you make.
Does the 30% apply to boosted posts too?
The 30% is on the sales your organic content drives. When we put ad budget behind a video in the Spotlight, we cover that spend — so those boosted sales aren't commissioned, but the extra reach & followers are all yours.
Is there any cost to me?
Never. Product is free at every tier and rewards grow as you do. We never ask you to pay.
